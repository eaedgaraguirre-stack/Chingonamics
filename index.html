The official story sells the premiere. We score the plant.

Southern California did not become worth crossing a border for because a studio had a good quarter. It became that because there were docks, assembly lines, tool rooms, ports, and shops that turned wages into houses, tuition, and a second generation that could aim higher than the first.

Hollywood is real. It is also a costume. Underneath it is one of the country’s largest manufacturing workforces, the busiest container complex in North America, and a South Bay aerospace spine that is hiring again. That is the body. This Index exists so the costume is not mistaken for the body.

Method, short version

Presence-weighted. Not headquarters-weighted. SpaceX can live in Texas on paper and still be a Hawthorne company in this book.

Composite, base year 2024 = 100:

  • 30% Jobs that pay — aero/defense, manufacturing, trade/transport, construction
  • 25% Metal and dirt — industrial absorption, plant permits, announced capex
  • 25% Contracts and capital — federal awards by place of performance, port TEUs, public hard-tech rounds
  • 20% The ladder — establishment births and apprenticeships when a public series exists

Gauges: Aerospace & Space · Plants · Ports & Movement · Building the Basin · Quiet Giants. Entertainment sits on a satellite strip called The Costume. It is never the cover.

Above 100 = stronger than 2024. Below 90 = the costume is doing more work than the body. First live score ships as Issue 00 / ESTIMATE.
