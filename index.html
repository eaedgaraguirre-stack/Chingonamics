About

Hollywood is cool. The real stars are in the region’s factories and workshops — docks, tool rooms, aerospace lots, and steel in the ground.

Hollywood is real. It is also a costume. Underneath it is one of the country’s largest manufacturing workforces, the busiest container complex in North America, and a South Bay aerospace floor that still matters.

L.A. isn’t only what’s on a screen — it’s the builders and disruptors on the lot who make this place amazing when the premiere’s over. The Index counts them.

Method

We score the plant. Not the letterhead. Presence-weighted — not headquarters-weighted. SpaceX can live in Texas on paper and still be a Hawthorne company in this book. Tips without a public source stay out of the file.

Why these weights (editorial judgments published in the open — disagree with the recipe, don’t guess at it). Composite, base year 2024 = 100:

  • 30% Jobs that pay — aero/defense, manufacturing, trade/transport, construction. Payroll is the clearest receipt a floor is real.
  • 25% Metal and dirt — industrial absorption, plant permits, announced capex. Capacity before or after the payroll print.
  • 25% Contracts and capital — federal awards by place of performance, port TEUs, public hard-tech rounds. Work landing here, not just letterhead.
  • 20% The ladder — establishment births and apprenticeships. Weighted lower because public series are spottier — not because the ladder matters less.

When Ladder data is thin: prefer a published series with clear geography and method. Missing or non-comparable Ladder inputs are excluded and the rest are reweighted inside the 20% sleeve. If the whole Ladder sleeve can’t be sourced, we hold the prior Issue’s Ladder contribution, mark the print provisional on Ladder, and say so in the Issue note. We never invent numbers. Estimates are labeled. Arithmetic is shown.

Gauges: Aerospace & Space · Plants · Ports & Movement · Building the Basin · Quiet Giants. Entertainment sits on a satellite strip called The Costume. Studios, streamers, celebrities, and cultural-heat strips never enter the scored composite.

Sources: BLS CES/QCEW · POLA/POLB TEUs · USAspending (place of performance) · public permits and capex. No source = not data.
